# Reactor Heart placeholder SFX

Launch.wav (0.23 s), Checkpoint.wav (0.35 s) and TurretFire.wav (0.13 s) are original deterministic mono synthesized clips, generated by `generate_reactor_sfx.py` using the existing standard-library synthesis helper. They contain no third-party samples. They are safe to ship as project-owned placeholder sounds.

22050 Hz / 16-bit / mono PCM. The scene's existing GameplayAudio component handles the three new events through its existing three voices. Existing gains, audio cooldowns and six cues are unchanged. The new voices do not persist across scene loads.
